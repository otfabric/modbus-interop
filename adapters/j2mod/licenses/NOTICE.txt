modbus-interop-j2mod: what the image contains and under which licence.

The exact jars of a build are listed in BUNDLED-JARS.txt (written by the image
build). They are the unmodified artifacts from Maven Central.

Component                                   Licence        Text
------------------------------------------  -------------  -----------------------------
modbus-interop adapter (this repository)    MIT            modbus-interop/LICENSE
com.ghgande:j2mod                           Apache-2.0     Apache-2.0.txt
com.fazecast:jSerialComm                    Apache-2.0 OR LGPL-3.0-or-later; used under Apache-2.0, Apache-2.0.txt
com.google.code.gson:gson                   Apache-2.0     Apache-2.0.txt
com.google.errorprone:error_prone_annotations  Apache-2.0  Apache-2.0.txt
org.slf4j:slf4j-api, slf4j-simple           MIT            slf4j-*/LICENSE.txt

Source code:
  https://github.com/steveohara/j2mod
  https://github.com/Fazecast/jSerialComm

jSerialComm is a dependency of j2mod (its serial transports); the adapter does
not use a serial port. j2mod's slf4j-reload4j binding is excluded from the
build: the library logs through slf4j-simple to stderr.

The Java runtime is Eclipse Temurin (GPL-2.0 WITH Classpath-exception-2.0) from
the base image; its licence and notices are in /opt/java/openjdk/legal and
/opt/java/openjdk/NOTICE.
