modbus-interop-modbus4j: what the image contains and under which licence.

This image is built locally and not published: modbus4j is GPL-3.0-or-later,
and so is the adapter that links it.

The exact jars of a build are listed in BUNDLED-JARS.txt (written by the image
build). modbus4j is built from its release source (modbus4j/SOURCE names the
archive, its SHA-256 and its commit); the others are the unmodified artifacts
from Maven Central.
